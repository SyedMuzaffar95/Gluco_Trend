What Training Does

  • Loads dataset/GlucoTrend_benchmark_dataset.csv with target glucose.
  • Builds leakage-safe features (identifiers, notes and the leaky rolling mean are removed; timestamps become time features).
  • Uses a chronological 80/20 train-test split.
  • Fits the preprocessing pipeline on training data only.
  • Trains Linear, Ridge, Decision Tree, Random Forest, KNN, SVR, Gradient Boosting and (when installed) XGBoost regressors.
  • Selects the best model by test R² and saves pipeline + metadata with joblib (atomic write).
